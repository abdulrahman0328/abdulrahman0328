# Data Visualization

**Role in my workflow:** Visualize → Explain → Support Decisions

My visualization approach is based on one principle:

> **A dashboard should answer a question, not simply display numbers.**

## Focus Areas

- KPI design
- Clear layouts
- Filtering
- Comparisons
- Trends
- Business context
- Visual hierarchy
- Decision-oriented reporting

## My Design Goal

Turn analytical results into visuals that help a stakeholder understand **what happened, why it matters, and what deserves attention**.
